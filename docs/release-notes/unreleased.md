# mtm, unreleased

Notes for the next release. CHANGELOG.md is generated by o-inventory; these lines are the hand-written part.

## v0.18.0 (unreleased)

- Pane and session copies are read from SQLite once per change instead of on every read: lookups during a frame (pane get and list, the doc store, a pane key) answer from an in-memory copy of the copies table that refreshes when the table's version (this process's writes, or SQLite's `data_version` for another process) moves, and a sync whose listing and copies are unchanged skips its per-copy pass. Results are unchanged; idle CPU drops.
- Needs you follows one rule (`inbox.needs-you-rule`, default `one`): a row stays while its item is open (not read, archived or snoozed), for every kind and source. Reading a row takes it out whatever it is (a waiting agent's, an errored one's, a failed command's, a Slack or GitHub item); mtm's own items of any kind (the private-project item) now show; an item whose pane is in Working, closed or hidden is a row of its own naming the pane. The panel title and the header's `N need you` count the same rows. `W` walks those rows in panel order, wrapping, and says `nothing needs you` when there are none; `ctrl+a W` in live view is the same step, attaching the row's pane (`panes.waiting-live-attach`, default on; off leaves live and selects). Attaching a pane marks every open item of it read at once, items raised while you watch are raised read, and a closed pane's command items clear as its agent items do (a failed one archived, a long one read). `legacy` restores the earlier rules.
- Fixed (owner, 2026-11-13: Needs you rows could not be cleared): the keys on a Needs-you row reach what it stands for again, for every row kind, through key-redirect rows that are data (`cmd/mtm/bindings/rows.json`, ochazuke's `unbound` pattern and `ochazuke.key.redirect`): on a pane row, a key the pane does not bind is its first item's, so `u` reads it, `x` archives it and `s` snoozes it, and the row leaves at once (since panes carried their items as child rows, `u` there was app undo); on an item row or a subagent row drawn under a pane, a key the row does not bind is the pane's (`*` stars the pane; on a subagent row `d d` kills it and `m` moves it, while `enter` still opens the subagent's card). Any row can be turned off or changed in `bindings.json` (`{"context": "pane/normal", "bindings": [{"key": "unbound", "unbind": true}]}`).
- Renamed: the `pane-child` noun is `subagent` (any harness's spawned agent): `subagent.open` (`enter`) and `subagent.copy` (`y`). A `bindings.json` row naming `pane-child` must be renamed.
- `panes.sort-clock` (default `entered`) is the one time every list sorts by after the category rank: when the row entered its state (an item when it was raised, reopened or woke from a snooze; a Working pane when its agent entered its state); `created` (an item's first raise, the earlier queue order) and `agent-state` (a pane's agent state time for every pane row) are the options. Action items record when they were reopened (`reopened_at`). `panes.stable-order`'s description now says what it does.

- The Panes rail triages. Context items have a `dismissed` state beside the star (`context-item dismiss` and `undismiss`, `-` and `+` on a context-item row, `*` stars as before; over a visual selection one history entry that `history-entry.undo` reverts). The rail's first section after Focus today is **New**: the pane's items neither starred nor dismissed, flat (not split by kind), newest first, open. **Starred** keeps the starred ones (a star outranks a dismissal), and the kind sections (Links, Files, ...) and This project hold only starred and new items. Dismissed items leave the rail: `rail.dismissed` `hide` (default) leaves them out, `collapsed` folds them under one **Dismissed (N)** heading at the end; `context-item list --where dismissed=true` lists them either way. `context-item dismiss --all` (or `--before 2026-11-14`, or `--filter WORDS`) dismisses every matching item in one store write, all stamped with one `dismissed-at`; its answer names the command that takes the batch back (`context-item undismiss --filter dismissed-at=STAMP`).
- **Needs you** on the rail and the Items page follow the panel's one rule (an item is in Needs you while it is open and in a `panes.queue-order` category; every other item is resolved) and draw two tables: Needs you, oldest first by `panes.sort-clock`, over **Resolved** (read, archived and snoozed items), newest first. Each row is labelled by what it asks (`waits for your input`, `` `go test` exited 1 after 3m``) rather than by its pane's name (the pane stays in the for column), and near-duplicates (same pane, source, kind and label) fold to one row with a count (`turn finished ×105`). The Items page does this as `inbox.group` `needs-you`, now the default (`owner`, `pane`, `project`, `source`, `state` and `none` remain); it folds from the inbox's cache, so it reads no extra table. On the rail Resolved starts folded and lists archived items too: `rail.archived` (default `action-item.resolved-pane`) names the rail sections that show archived things whatever `collection.hide-archived` says for other lists.
- Every rail row starts with a one-cell glyph of its kind (`rail.icons`: `symbols` default, `nerd` for Nerd Font glyphs, `letters`, `off`), from one table by noun and context-item type.
- New filter-only props: `context-item` `kept-pane`, `new-pane`, `starred-pane`, `dismissed-pane`; `action-item` `needs-pane`, `resolved-pane` (each `--where PROP=PANE`). `rail.hidden` now defaults to `context-item.window, action-item.pane` (the untriaged lists those sections replace), `rail.groups` gains `new`, `starred`, `resolved` and `dismissed`, and `rail.collapsed` keeps New and Starred open.

- The Panes page has a rail: a column right of the pane's detail (ochazuke v0.134.0's rail) listing what the shown pane printed, asked and is tied to: the pane in live view, the side pane while its terminal holds the focus, else the selected row's pane. Its sections are data: the pane's context items split by type (Links, Files, Dirs, PRs, Branches, Commits, Commands), **Needs you** (action items now; o-questions decisions and questions and o-cmds commands join when those tools declare a pane prop) and **This project** (the rest of the pane's project's context items) as groups, notes and relations about the pane, and **Focus today** first, open, whatever pane is shown (empty: not drawn; there is no ticker). Headings fold to a count, a picker of kinds first; Needs you and Focus today start open (`rail.collapsed`). Rows are each noun's own rows with its own keys. `Z` shows or hides it (`ochazuke.rail.toggle`; `ctrl+a Z` in live view, through the leader fall-through); `}`/`tab`/`ctrl+a o` enter it, `{` leaves, `j`/`k` stay at its ends, `h`/`l` fold and unfold, `ctrl+w >`/`<` resize it. The live terminal is sized to the width the rail leaves. Where the detail is too narrow for the column (a detail under about 89 columns), it steps down to 28 cells, then to a float over the detail's top-right corner, then to a line of counts. Over a zoomed pane it floats (`rail.zoom`: `float` default, `full`, `none`). `rail.show` (default `true`) turns it off: off, it draws and reads nothing. The other rail settings are ochazuke's (`rail.look` default `full`, `rail.width` `25%`, `rail.min-main` `60`, `rail.order`, `rail.hidden`, `rail.groups`, `rail.empty` `hide`, `rail.counts`, `rail.refresh`, `rail.ends` `stay`). For the pane's own context alone, add `this-project` to `rail.hidden`. With the rail on, `j`/`k` on the Panes tree measured 8.8 ms per key and frame (median, 200 presses, 240x50) against 7.5 ms with it off; drawing reads no store.

- A registered tool whose noun has its own `watch` verb (o-md's `doc`, o-inventory's `daily`) now streams it: mtm runs `TOOL NOUN watch --json {...}` for as long as the watch lasts and passes each JSON line on, so `inventory daily watch` and a rail or list watching a mounted noun get the tool's events as they happen.

## persistent tool connections (ptc-mtm: ptc-4, ptc-5)

- Added: mtm keeps a registered tool running when its catalog declares ochazuke's host serve protocol (`"serve": 1`, ochazuke v0.135.0's `cli.Serves()`), one `cli.ServePool` per mounted extension, and sends it the calls it made by exec: a mounted verb (`ProxyCommand.Pool`), its canonical-key preflight, the page loads (`Fetch`, `FetchRecord`, `FetchFlags`), the parameter choices and a noun's watch (`cli.PoolWatch`). Results and errors are the same bytes as an exec's. No registered tool declares the protocol yet, so every call still execs until each tool is rebuilt with it.
- Added settings (Settings › Extensions › General): `extensions.transport` (`persistent` default, `exec` is one process per call as before), `extensions.persistent-after` (2: the first call to a tool in a process execs, so a one-shot command never starts a server), `extensions.pool-size` (2), `extensions.idle-timeout` (5m, 0 never), `extensions.pool-wait` (250ms: how long a read waits for a kept process before it takes another or execs; reads share all but one of the processes, so a page's loads run one after another on a running tool; 0 never waits), `extensions.host-calls` (`pipe` default: a kept tool's `Host.Call` and `HostCommand` are answered in this mtm process on a headless runtime of their own, built once, never the TUI's; `exec` starts mtm for each, as before).
- Added: the `extension-connection` noun, one row per kept process (extension, pid, state, calls, started, last error), with `all`, `show`, `restart` (drains the extension's processes: the next call starts fresh and serving is tried again after a failure) and `yank` (the pid); `extension reconnect [NAME]` shares restart's handler. No keys.
- Changed: a kept process whose binary was rebuilt, or whose catalog differs from the registered one, is drained and the catalog check runs, so a reinstalled tool is used on the next call.
- Changed: a shared bare extension noun word follows ochazuke's `cli.ambiguous` (`Runtime.SharedNoun`): `host` (default) and `ask` refuse it with ochazuke's message naming each noun in full (`"doc" is ambiguous: json.doc, md.doc (write one in full, or set cli.ambiguous to first-registered)`, which replaces mtm's own wording), `first-registered` gives it to the extension registered first. Changing the setting renames at once.
- deps: ochazuke v0.135.0.

## the day's focus (focus-2-mtm, mtm-focus-parity)

- Added: the `focus-item` noun, the things you picked as the day's focus. A focus is its own dated mark (it leaves the task's status alone), kept in the new `focus_items` table. Today's list is computed on read: the day's own picks in order, then (setting `focus.carry`, default `unfinished`; `none` starts each day empty) earlier days' unfinished ones; a task finished today stays in today's list, flagged `finished`, and drops off the next day. Nothing runs at midnight.
- Added verbs: `focus-item add|remove|new|move|clear|open|next|previous` (with `--date YYYY-MM-DD`, default today) beside the standard `get|list|show|history|watch|yank|archive|restore`; `task focus` and `outline-task focus` (toggle; `--value` sets), with `unfocus` aliases; `task new --focus`. `focus-item move` takes `+N`, `-N` or a place `N`.
- Added: task props `focused` and `focus-order` (read-only, derived) and the saved filter `focus`: `mtm task list --filter focus` lists today's focus in order.
- Added keys: `=` on a task row (Work page and the `T` outline) focuses or unfocuses it; `#` anywhere opens the focus picker (both keys were free in mtm and every registered tool; `=` is the unshifted `+`; `F` is fetch or refresh across the family and `+` adds a column in o-csv): today's focus first (◎), then the open tasks, the pinned project's first; enter toggles and the picker stays open; a typed title that matches nothing offers "+ focus new task". `ctrl+a #` in live view reaches it too. A task made from inside a modal no longer switches to the Work page.
- Changed: a tool's row key now wins over one of mtm's app-wide keys on the tool's own rows, as the framework's app keys already did (motions and captured rows stay mtm's). So a tool's own key (`F` fetch or refresh) stays its own on its rows.

## parity: every tool verb mounts, shell.init (mtm-focus-parity)

- Changed: a registered tool's verbs mount whatever their kind. mtm dropped the ones a tool runs at once on its loop ("interaction" verbs, 37 of o-csv's), a leftover from before catalogs marked the framework's verbs; both kinds run the same way, as a fresh run of the tool. A verb that needs the tool's own screen answers the tool's refusal ("not applicable now"). Nouns pinned to a mode (o-csv's prompt `field`) still do not mount: they live in a modal of the tool's screen.
- Added: `shell.init NAME` (`zsh` or `bash`) answers the rc-file snippet of `mtm shell-init`, so the palette (in the text view, to copy), MCP and bindings reach it. `mtm shell-init zsh` still prints the raw snippet for an rc file; `mtm shell init zsh` is now that verb and answers JSON, as every verb does.

## less lag after each action (mtm-action-lag)

- Changed: the Items list (action-item store) is read again only when what it shows changes: its items, the grouping, a pane, project or task name it shows, or a snooze ending. A task toggled on Work, or 5 s passing, no longer re-reads it on every page; the ages in its `when` column are redrawn from the rows on the inbox timer. A task toggle on a copy of real data went from about 0.85 s to about 0.13 s.
- Changed: the grouped Items query reads pane and project names from two lookup tables built once per query instead of searching a JSON argument for each row, and the store projects each row once (a materialized, JSONB projection) instead of once per read of it: about 650 ms to about 70 ms over 3,645 items. - Built on ochazuke v0.58.0. A visual-line delete over several rows names the first row and counts the rest ("Delete task write the brief and 1 more?"), with what they orphan; v0.57.0 said "Delete task true?".
- Renamed: an extension's nouns mount as `<ext>.<noun>` (ochazuke's `cli.Namespace`, as every family host names them), no longer `<ext>-<noun>`: `md-doc` is `md.doc`, `json-doc` is `json.doc`, and so on for every mounted noun. Their verbs keep their names: `md-doc.send-notes` is `md.doc.send-notes`.
- Added: a noun no other mounted extension has also answers to its bare name (`item` for a `fake.item` no other extension shares), in the palette, the CLI (`mtm item list`), keys and `bindings.json`, and is drawn by it. A name two extensions share (`doc` for `json.doc` and `md.doc`) gets no bare alias; the palette offers both full names (ochazuke `palette.noun-tails`). The namespace words work too: `mtm md doc list`, `:md doc list`.
- Deprecated: the old spelling `<ext>-<noun>` stays an alias of the new noun until after v0.3.0. A `bindings.json` row, an `actions.json` row or a script that names it still runs, with one notice (`md-doc is an old name of md.doc (renamed in v0.2.0; removed after v0.3.0)`); `mtm bindings check` names each row spelled the old way.
- New setting `extensions.noun-names` (Settings › Extensions › General): `namespaced` (the default, above) or `legacy` (`<ext>-<noun>` only, no aliases, as before). Changing it remounts the extensions.
- An extension whose name ochazuke reserves as a namespace (a framework noun's short name such as `list` or `settings`, `app`, `ochazuke`) keeps the dashed spelling, with a notice.

## Schedules count from the finish

- New schedule field `interval_from` (`interval-from` in `schedule edit|set|create` and the form): `start` (the default; an old row reads as `start`) or `finish`. A `finish` row's next run is due one interval after the last run finished, so a slow call no longer shortens the gap; a run in flight is never due and shows no next time. An invalid value is refused like other fields. Backoff after failures applies from the finish too.
- The built-in `gh-signals` and `slack-signals` rows default to `finish`. A `schedules.json` row naming them changes only the fields it gives, so it inherits `finish` unless it sets `interval_from`; a stored copy made before this release keeps the value it was saved with (empty reads as `start`) until you set `interval-from finish`. No user config is rewritten. `last_finished` is kept in the schedule's state; a state row without it counts from the start until the next run.
- Fixed: an idle mtm used a core when panes were busy. Every burst of pane output woke the loop with a message (a full update and a redraw each, 50 a second with a dozen panes); wake-ups now come at most once per second (`contextBatch`), and the output still marks its panes dirty for the follow job. Test: `TestFollowWakesTheLoopAtMostOncePerPace`.

## Task outline

- Task statuses come from the `tasks.statuses` setting: name, the one character drawn inside the checkbox brackets, rank and closed. The default adds `waiting` `[~]` and `cancelled` `[-]` beside todo, active `[>]`, blocked `[!]` and done `[x]`; edit the setting to change glyphs or add statuses. Existing tasks keep their status names; a name the table lacks draws `[?]`. `outline-task.status`, `task.edit` and `task set status=` take any name in the table.
- On an outline task row `t a w b c` set todo, active, waiting, blocked and cancelled (`x` is still done, `s` still picks). Over a visual selection each key sets every selected task as one undo step. They are binding rows: rebind or add keys for your own statuses in `bindings.json` with `outline-task.status` and `status`.
- The lines under the outline title list the focused row's keys from its bindings, so `J K g J g K` and the status keys show. `tasks.outline-hint-lines` (default 4; `0` keeps the old fixed text) sets how many; a short terminal shows fewer.
- `m` over a visual selection moves every selected task to the chosen project, and `u` (then `ctrl+r`) undoes the move as one step.
- `C` on a task row picks a context item (the task's project's and the unassociated ones; `S` for every project's) and attaches it to the task (`outline-task.attach-context`; `outline-task.attach --context-item|--action-item|--pane` is `task.associate` on the row). `N` writes a note about the task under the cursor.
- A new blank row draws its `[ ]` at the row's indent instead of at the far right (ochazuke v0.126.1).
- From ochazuke v0.126.0: the `?` binding help follows every bindings change without reopening, and `b` captures a key sequence (`B` captures until `enter`); `help.bind-sequences` (default true) turns that off.
- A key now finds the thing it acts on by walking the relation graph, up to `keys.graph-depth` hops (default 3) and `keys.graph-fan-out` candidates per hop (default 20), instead of one hop of live associations. From a live terminal a key can reach the pane's project. When several things qualify, the verb asks which; a leader key that cannot ask shows a notice and runs nothing. A dead outward reference in the walk stays a row instead of failing it. (ochazuke v0.127.0.)
- `mtm shell-init zsh|bash` prints the rc-file snippet that makes a pane's shell send OSC 133 marks (prompt start and end, command start, command end with its exit status), which `pane run`, terminal-command inbox items and shell turns read. It does nothing without `MTM_ENV=1`, keeps your prompt and hooks, is idempotent, and needs no other tool's script. Use `eval "$(mtm shell-init zsh)"`, or cache its output in a file (it is stable for one mtm version). `mtm shell init zsh` is the same command.
